Who We Are

Welcome to CAT Corp.

As the parent company of GANZ Industries, C.O.R.E. and Halberd & Hems, CAT operates from the shadows, orchestrating innovation, control, and strategic development across multiple sectors.

While each subsidiary maintains its own identity and public mission, their true directive aligns with CAT's long-term vision: to shape the unseen future of global power, technology, and influence.
